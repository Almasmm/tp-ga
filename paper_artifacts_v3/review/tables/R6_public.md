# FCTP: public instances (subset of the test problems of Sun et al. used by Glover et al. 2005)

Exploratory (amendment 10, written after the results of amendments 8 and 9 were seen). Subset of the test problems of Sun, Aronson, McKeown and Drinka (1998) used by Glover, Amini and Kochenberger (2005); files of https://github.com/Tybirk/RL-FCTP, directory Java/Glover, commit 1ab1ba073363c7b650fab7ef1f93b8a7755ba2cb. Published values: Glover, Amini, Kochenberger, Journal of Heuristics 11 (2005) 307-336, Table 3. `published_*`: CPLEX 9.0, tabu search of Sun et al. and the parametric ghost image process, as printed there; `barr2021`: the value of Barr et al. (Networks, 2021, Table 2), which differs for a reason we do not know. `mip_*`: our HiGHS run (1 thread, 60 s). z_ref: the optimum proved by our MIP, otherwise the smallest of the published and of our values. Methods: 10 seeds, B = 200 m n; gap of the median cost to z_ref.
