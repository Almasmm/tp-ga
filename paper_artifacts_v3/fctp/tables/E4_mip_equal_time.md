# FCTP: the MIP solver with the time of the GA (amendment 9.3)

T of an instance is the median run time of GA-cost-cycle over its 10 runs (20 runs at once). The MIP (HiGHS, one thread) is run with the limit T, with a multiple of T and with 60 s; its plan is compared with the median cost of GA-cost-cycle over the seeds. `ga_better_or_no_plan` includes the instances where the MIP has no plan at the limit. Descriptive: the times depend on the machine and on the load; no test is made.
